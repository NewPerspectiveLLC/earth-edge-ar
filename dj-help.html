🎙 How to talk to everyoneLive DJ voice · Live Geometry

Your voice goes out live over the music to everyone in Live Geometry. It comes from the flying-sound orb, and the music ducks while you talk. Each session is saved, so you can repeat or replay it later.

1 · Open the Master DJ page

Master DJ on this site (dj.html) Cell / anywhere (tunnel) Home Wi-Fi (LAN) GitHub Pages copy Inside the app you can also tap the 🎧 Now playing pill to open the same DJ panel. It has a 🎙 Talk to everyone (live) section.

2 · Enter the GM key (first time only)

On dj.html, type the key into “GM key (same as the GM page)” and tap Unlock controls. In the in-app DJ panel the field says “GM key (asked once on this device)” → Save.
The key is stored only in this browser on this device (its local storage, shared with gm.html and the in-app panel on the same site), so you won't be asked again. Lock on dj.html hides the controls again. Private browsing, or clearing website data, forgets the key. Each address (cell / LAN / Pages) remembers it separately.

3 · Allow the microphone

The mic opens only when you tap 🎙 Go live. Safari then asks “… would like to access the microphone”: tap Allow.
  • Denied by mistake? In Safari tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Microphone: Allow, then reload.
  • Or iPhone Settings → Apps → Safari → Microphone (older iOS: Settings → Safari → Microphone) → Ask or Allow.
  • The page must be opened over https:// (all links above are).

4 · Go live, talk, stop

  1. Tap 🎙 Live voice & broadcasts… on dj.html (or scroll to 🎙 Talk to everyone (live) in the in-app panel).
  2. Optional: choose then repeat (no, 1× … 10×) and gap (1 to 60 s) for an automatic replay after you stop.
  3. Tap 🎙 Go live and talk. The button turns into ⏹ End live (on air N s).
  4. Tap ⏹ End live when done. The session is saved, and repeats play if you chose them.
While you talk, everyone's music drops to about 20 % and comes back smoothly afterwards. Listeners hear your voice coming from the glowing orb.

5 · Replay, repeat, stop for everyone

  • 🔁 Replay last (m:ss): plays the last saved session to everyone again, using the repeat and gap you picked.
  • ⛔ Stop broadcast now: stops a live session or a replay for everyone right away.
  • The status line shows 🔴 Someone is live now, 📢 Broadcast playing for everyone and Live delay here ≈ X s.

6 · What listeners need

  • Live Geometry open on screen (phones pause web audio when locked or in the background).
  • Sound on: ringer/silent switch off, volume up. On iPhone, tap once (Start AR, or 🎵 Music) so Safari allows audio.
  • Meta Quest: audio starts automatically after Enter VR (it counts as the tap).
  • Flying sound / music on. The voice is mixed into the orb's sound.

7 · Delay & troubleshooting

  • Expect about 1 second of delay over cell, the tunnel and Pages (measured ~0.96 s), and less on home Wi-Fi.
  • “Enter the GM key first”: do step 2.
  • Mic error / nothing on air: check step 3, close other apps using the mic (calls, voice memos), reload.
  • Listeners hear nothing: they need step 6, especially the one tap on iPhone. Ask them to reload once.
  • Echo or feedback: use earbuds, or turn down the DJ page's own sound (don't tap 🔈 Listen here on the device you talk from).
  • “server not reachable”: the Mac or tunnel is down. Try the LAN link at home.
